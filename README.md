# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

The Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, payment trends, and category-wise profitability using retail sales data.

This dashboard helps in identifying key business insights through dynamic visualizations, KPI tracking, and trend analysis.

---

## Features

* Interactive KPI Cards for:

  * Total Sales Amount
  * Total Profit
  * Total Quantity Sold
  * Average Order Value (AOV)

* Monthly Profit Trend Analysis

* Category-wise Quantity Distribution

* State-wise Sales Analysis

* Payment Mode Analysis

* Customer-wise Sales Contribution

* Dynamic Filters and Slicers

* Business Insights Section

---

## Technologies Used

* Power BI
* SQL Concepts
* Excel / CSV Dataset
* Data Visualization
* Power Query

---

## Dashboard Components

### KPI Cards

Displays important business metrics such as:

* Sales Amount
* Profit
* Quantity
* Average Order Value

### Profit Trend Analysis

Analyzes monthly profit fluctuations and identifies peak and low-performing periods.

### Category Analysis

Visualizes product category contribution and sub-category profitability.

### Payment Mode Insights

Shows customer payment preferences using donut chart visualization.

### Customer Analysis

Highlights top contributing customers based on purchase amount.

---

## Key Business Insights

* Clothing category contributed the highest quantity sold.

* COD emerged as the most preferred payment method among customers.

* Maharashtra generated the highest sales amount.

* Printers and Bookcases were the most profitable sub-categories.

* Profit experienced decline during specific months indicating seasonal fluctuation.

## Project Learnings

* Developed an interactive dashboard to analyze retail sales and customer trends.
